    What to eat more of, and less of

      Supplements with strong evidence

      • Creatine monohydrate, 3–5 g/day. Most-studied supplement for strength and lean mass. Take it any time of day; no loading phase needed.
      • Protein powder only as a convenience to reach your protein target. Food first.
      • Caffeine, 3–6 mg/kg about 60 min before hard sessions can improve performance. Avoid it within ~8 hours of bed.
      • Vitamin D if blood levels are low or you get little sun (common in winter at northern latitudes).
      • Omega-3 (EPA/DHA) if you eat oily fish less than twice a week.

      Check with your doctor before starting supplements if you take medication or have a medical condition.

      Your heart-rate zones

      Sleep
      • Adults need 7–9 hours; short sleep blunts muscle recovery and raises hunger hormones.
      • Dark, cool room (around 18 °C), screens away 30–60 min before bed.
      • Get 10+ minutes of outdoor light soon after waking to anchor your body clock.
      Stress & recovery
      • If resting heart rate is 5+ bpm above normal, or sleep and mood dip for days, take an easy day.
      • 5–10 min of slow breathing (around 6 breaths/min) lowers stress and blood pressure.
      • Alcohol worsens sleep quality and muscle repair. Less is better; no amount is risk-free.
      Know your numbers
      • Blood pressure: aim for under 120/80 mmHg. Check it at least yearly.
      • Ask your doctor about cholesterol, blood glucose (HbA1c) and age-appropriate screening.
      • Stop and get checked if exercise brings chest pain, fainting, or unusual breathlessness.
